THE SCHOOL

BPS is situated near Barsoi Block- Zamhira Road. It was established by Md.Naiyer Alam in 2001 and it is managed by the Pvt. Unaided. It is located in Rural area. It is located in BARSOI block of KATIHAR district of Bihar. It is the largest convent of this area. The school consists of Grades from 1 to 8.

The school has a nice magnificent building. The school has electric connection. The source of Drinking Water in the school is Hand Pumps and it is functional. The school has 6 boys toilet and it is functional, and 3 girls toilet and it is functional. The school has a library and has 1000 books in its library.
