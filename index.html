ABOU THE BARSOI PUBLIC SCHOOL

Established in 2001 by Md. Naiyer Alam, Barsoi Public School (BPS) stands as the premier and largest co-educational English-medium convent school in the Barsoi block of Katihar district, Bihar. Situated near the Barsoi Block–Zamhira Road, the institution offers quality education from Grades 1 to 8 in a vibrant, supportive, and accessible rural environment. Managed privately, the school operates during standard daytime hours and commences its academic sessions every April, ensuring a structured, focused, and value-driven foundation for over 600 students..

The school features a well-maintained, magnificent campus equipped with 8 spacious, light-filled classrooms, dedicated administrative offices, and a secure boundary wall. BPS provides a complete learning setup, including a full library with over 1,000 books, functional computer facilities, clean drinking water, separate functional sanitation facilities for boys and girls, and electricity connection. Beyond academics, a large playground hosts daily sports and recreational activities. Supported by a team of 20 highly qualified, caring teachers and 6 dedicated staff members, BPS is committed to nurturing academic excellence, discipline, and character building in every child.
